# Returns and Refunds Policy

Applies to all NovaMart stores and novamart.example orders. Last updated: 2026-09-01.

## Standard return window
Most items can be returned within **30 days** of the purchase date (in-store) or the delivery date (online orders). The item must be in resalable condition with all original accessories. A receipt or order number is required for a refund to the original payment method.

## Electronics
Electronics (laptops, tablets, phones, TVs, cameras, game consoles) have a shorter **15-day** return window. Opened electronics are accepted if all accessories and packaging are included. A **15% restocking fee** applies to opened laptops and TVs unless the item is defective.

## Non-returnable items
The following cannot be returned: gift cards, opened personal care products, underwear and swimwear with hygiene liners removed, perishable food, and clearance items marked "Final Sale".

## Returns without a receipt
Without a receipt or order number, the customer may receive **store credit** at the item's lowest selling price in the last 60 days. A government-issued photo ID is required. Limit: three no-receipt returns per customer per 12 months.

## Refund timing
Refunds to credit or debit cards are issued immediately at the register but can take **5–10 business days** to appear on the customer's statement. Online returns are refunded within 3 business days after the warehouse receives the item.

## Holiday return extension
Items purchased between **November 1 and December 24** can be returned until **January 31** of the following year. The electronics window is extended the same way. This extension does not apply to Final Sale items.
