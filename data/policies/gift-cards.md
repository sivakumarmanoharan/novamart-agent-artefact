# Gift Card Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-12.

## Purchase limits
Gift cards can be loaded with **$10 to $500**. Purchases totalling more than **$2,000** in a single transaction require store manager approval (fraud prevention).

## Expiry and fees
NovaMart gift cards do not expire and have no inactivity fees.

## Refunds and cash out
Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by provincial law. In Quebec, customers can request cash for a remaining balance under **$5**.

## Lost or stolen cards
A lost card can be replaced only if the customer has the original purchase receipt or the card is registered online. The remaining balance is moved to a new card.

## Scams
Never help a customer buy gift cards if they say they are paying a government agency, utility, or a stranger who contacted them. Politely explain this is a common scam and alert the store manager.
