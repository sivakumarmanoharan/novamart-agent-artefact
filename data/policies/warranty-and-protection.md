# Warranty and NovaCare Protection Plans

Last updated: 2026-03-10.

## Manufacturer warranty
All new products carry the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year. Within the return window, defective items are exchanged in store. After the return window, customers are directed to the manufacturer, except for NovaMart private-label brands (NovaHome, Brightline), which NovaMart services directly for 1 year.

## NovaCare protection plans
NovaCare is NovaMart's extended protection plan for electronics and major appliances. Plans are available in **2-year** and **3-year** terms and must be purchased within **30 days** of buying the product. NovaCare covers mechanical and electrical failures and, for laptops, tablets and phones, **one accidental damage claim** (drops and spills) per plan term.

## What NovaCare does not cover
Cosmetic damage that does not affect function, lost or stolen items, intentional damage, software issues, and consumable parts such as batteries after 12 months.

## Making a claim
Customers start a claim in store or by calling NovaCare at 1-800-555-0199. Bring the product and receipt. If the item cannot be repaired within **10 business days**, the customer receives a replacement of equal or similar specification.

## Cancelling a plan
A NovaCare plan can be cancelled for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, the refund is prorated and no refund is given if a claim has been made.
