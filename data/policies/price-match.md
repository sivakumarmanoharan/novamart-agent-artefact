# Price Match Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-15.

## Eligibility
NovaMart matches the current advertised price of an **identical item** (same brand, model number, size and colour) that is in stock at a qualifying competitor. The customer must show the competitor's price on a phone or in a printed ad.

## Qualifying competitors
Qualifying competitors are major national retailers with a physical store in Canada and their own websites. Marketplace sellers (third-party sellers on Amazon, eBay, Walmart Marketplace, etc.) do **not** qualify.

## Exclusions
We do not match: Black Friday and Cyber Monday doorbuster prices, clearance or open-box prices, membership-only prices (e.g. Costco), bundle offers, financing offers, or prices that include a coupon.

## Price match after purchase
If NovaMart lowers its own price within **14 days** of purchase, the customer can request a refund of the difference with the receipt. This also applies if a qualifying competitor lowers the price within 14 days. Limit: one adjustment per item.

## Who can approve
Associates can approve price matches up to **$200** difference. Larger differences require a store manager's approval.
