# NovaRewards Loyalty Program

Last updated: 2026-05-01.

## Earning points
Members earn **1 point per $1** spent on eligible purchases, before tax. Gold members earn **1.5 points per $1**. Gift card purchases and NovaCare plans do not earn points.

## Tiers
- **Member**: free to join.
- **Gold**: reached after spending **$1,500** in a calendar year. Gold status lasts until the end of the following calendar year. Gold members get free express shipping.

## Redeeming points
**1,000 points = $10** off a purchase. Points can be redeemed in blocks of 1,000, in store or online. Points cannot be redeemed for gift cards or cash.

## Points expiry
Points expire after **12 months** without any account activity (earning or redeeming points).

## Returns and points
When an item is returned, the points earned on it are removed. If the points have already been spent, the value is deducted from the refund.

## Missing points
Customers can claim missing points within **60 days** of purchase with a receipt, at any customer service desk.
