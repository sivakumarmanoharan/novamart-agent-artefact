# Shipping, Delivery and Order Status Guide

Last updated: 2026-08-20.

## Shipping options
- **Standard shipping**: 3–7 business days. Free on orders over **$50**, otherwise $7.99.
- **Express shipping**: 1–2 business days, $19.99. Not available to remote postal codes.
- **Buy online, pick up in store (BOPIS)**: usually ready within 2 hours. Items are held for **7 days** before the order is cancelled and refunded.

## Order status meanings
- **PROCESSING**: payment approved, the warehouse has not yet shipped the order. Orders can still be cancelled at this stage.
- **SHIPPED / IN_TRANSIT**: with the carrier; a tracking number is available.
- **DELIVERED**: the carrier confirmed delivery.
- **DELAYED**: the carrier reported an exception (weather, address issue, lost scan).
- **CANCELLED**: the order was cancelled and a refund was issued.

## Cancelling or changing an order
Orders can only be cancelled while in PROCESSING. Once shipped, the customer must wait for delivery and then return the item.

## Late or missing packages
If an order is DELAYED for more than **5 business days** past its estimated delivery date, or marked DELIVERED but the customer did not receive it, open a carrier trace in the Ops Portal. If the trace is not resolved in **3 business days**, the customer may choose a replacement or a full refund.

## Damaged on arrival
Customers must report damage within **48 hours** of delivery with photos. Associates can process a free replacement without the customer returning the damaged item if its value is under $100.
