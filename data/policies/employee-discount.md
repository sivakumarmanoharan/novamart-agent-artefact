# Employee Discount Policy

Internal. Last updated: 2026-01-05.

## Eligibility
All active employees are eligible from their first day. Part-time and seasonal employees are included. Contractors and agency staff are not eligible.

## Discount amount
- **20%** off regular-priced merchandise.
- **10%** off electronics, gift cards excluded.
- The discount does not stack with clearance prices, price matches, or promotional coupons; the employee receives whichever price is lower.

## Annual limit
The discount applies to up to **$3,000** of purchases per calendar year (pre-discount value).

## Family members
Employees may register one household member, who receives the same discount. The employee is responsible for their household member's use of the discount.

## Rules
Employees must not ring up their own purchases; another associate must process the transaction. Buying items for resale is prohibited and leads to removal of the discount and disciplinary action.
